Our story

Blue Tide began with a simple idea: serve the sea's best catch with honesty and respect. Our restaurant background and mission statement tell why we cook the way we do.

What guides our kitchen

Freshness

Ingredients arrive daily from the boats and local markets. Every dish is prepared to order so the flavour of the sea stays bright.

Community

We work with local suppliers and fishing families, keeping money and skills within our coastal community.

Respect the sea

We choose responsible sourcing and share where our ingredients come from, so guests can eat with confidence.
